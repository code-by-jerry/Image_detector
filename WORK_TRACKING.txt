================================================================================
1. PROJECT SUMMARY
================================================================================

Milk Mirror is a Flutter app that analyzes rear udder photos of buffalo to
estimate daily milk production (1–30 L/day). The pipeline combines:

  - Rules-based veterinary gate (reject humans, laptops, non-livestock)
  - Milk Mirror geometric measurement (pin bones + escutcheon)
  - On-device TFLite band classifier (6_lit … 10_lit)
  - Optional scientific udder trait extraction (9-stage pipeline)
  - Firebase Firestore for capture metadata and continuous learning
  - Python training scripts for model retrain and export

Current runtime mode: Firestore-only (Firebase Storage disabled).
